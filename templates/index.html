        2. Settings

        Kind of place
        Procedural town

        Build a plausible invented town locally. Organic streets use spaced, irregular blocks and curved, connected routes; named roads follow the hierarchy through junctions. Homes follow street-front parcels with varied setbacks and yards. Shops and apartments cluster around town and neighborhood centers; industry sits near the edge and major roads. Rivers and parks shape the blocks. This is a procedural layout, not a reconstruction from local survey, terrain or elevation data. For real streets and mapped land cover, use normal OpenStreetMap mode. Saved parameters can be changed and applied again later.

        Larger buildings need larger blocks. The Workshop catalog has a wide range of lot sizes; this limit and block size determine which lots can fit safely. The Advanced largest-building setting can cap it further.

        More settings
        Building Pool V3

        Optional for both OSM maps and procedural towns: turn on Building Pool V3 templates under More settings. In OSM maps, an unchanged Workshop lot is placed inside a fully supported rectangular part of a building footprint; irregular areas that cannot fit safely still use KnoxMap's generator. The Build result reports how many lots were used. Your files stay on your computer. KnoxMap also checks assets/workshop/ in its project folder and Steam's steamapps/workshop/content/108600/. Building Pool V3 is a community collection on the Steam Workshop (item 2790726238); credit belongs to its author and contributing creators.
